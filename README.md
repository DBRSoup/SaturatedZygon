# Zygon

Adds two new exotic Photon Disc upgrades that encourage the use of weapons that can apply fire, shock, and acid in quick succession.

Designed to work independently, while providing synergies if used together.

## Saturated Zygon

Apply 10 stacks of fire, shock and acid from a single non-disc weapon within 10 seconds to trigger Saturation, increasing unattuned damage. Killing with element damage adds reduced stacks. 

While Zygon Frenzy is equipped, elements no longer stack Saturated Zygon while Saturation or Frenzy is active. Buffs also remain active during Frenzy.

## Zygon Frenzy

Apply 10 stacks of fire, shock, and acid with discs within 10 seconds to trigger Frenzy, increasing unattuned damage. Frenzy cannot retrigger while a Zygon buff is active unless Waning. 

While Saturated Zygon is equipped, discs no longer stack Zygon Frenzy outside of Waning and it now gains stacks from kills with element damage.

### Waning

Activates after Saturation or Frenzy expires. During Waning, only Saturation buffs remain active and elements can stack Saturated Zygon and Zygon Frenzy again.

### Addendum

The game does not exactly have a solid term for the amount of element applied by a source of damage, so the mod describes them as stacks. Stacks do not refer to the full application of an element that triggers its DoT effect, but the amount that is applied by a shot, for example:

* Decay Plates applying 10 ELEMENT, which would be 10 stacks of decay, which instantly trigger the Decay effect
* Elemental Munitions applying 3-6 AMOUNT, which would be 3-6 stacks of the given element

Kills with element damage are not kills done by the DoT of an element, but kills done by a bullet or other source of damage that has that element applied to it. This makes it so that weapons that shoot a few strong bullets instead of hosing enemies with statuses still have a chance to stack Zygon buffs, as long as the damage of the bullet that kills the enemy is one of the three.

Originally designed with upgrades such as Gunship Cannon's Elemental Munitions or Globbler's Volatile Element in mind, to allow upgrades that do not work with Syzygy to be more than just a straight damage nerf as they don't work with grenade damage improvements and upgrades like Focus Prism. 

Zygon upgrades also work with the likes of Cyclotron and Ralph's Conundrum, even though they already work with Syzygy, as the upgrades are only meant to increase unattuned damage. You can probably trigger the buffs with your player damage as well, using upgrades such as Splash Zone, Dynamite on a String, and Elemental Cocktail (the secondary effect's stacks are credited to the player instead of the weapon that triggered the primary element).
